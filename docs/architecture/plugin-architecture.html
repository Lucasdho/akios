Audit flags — candidates worth a closer look

  • Meta-documentation overlap. akios/Context.md, README.md, ios-agentic-kit, and ios-feature-pipeline all describe "how the kit works" from slightly different angles. Check whether all four earn their keep.
  • swiftui-* sub-skill sprawl. Six sub-skills under swift-dev/skills/ cover overlapping SwiftUI territory with no documented boundary in the router table. 627 lines vs. 95–108 in neighbors suggests uneven scope, not necessarily redundancy.
  • Vendored-looking guides inside an "authored skills" repo. swift-concurrency-pro and swift-testing-pro ship their own CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md — the repo's own .gitignore comment says vendored skills shouldn't be bundled here. Confirm intentional exception vs. drift.
  • align-ui sits outside akios/workflow.yml's phases: list. Well-referenced (30+ files) but declared only in prose (commands/design.md, ui-variations), not as its own phase/run-style. Likely fine (it's a gate inside design) — confirm intended shape.
  • commands/review.md is a two-line wrapper around a built-in. Confirm the doctrine pre-load still justifies a dedicated command.
  • tasks.md (root) is self-flagged in akios/Context.md as a legacy single-file backlog, "most tasks already done" — known removal candidate.
